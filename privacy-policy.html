PureBreath Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

PureBreath is a smoking cessation and cigarette-tracking application developed by SP Agency. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

When you use PureBreath, we may collect:

  • Account information, including your name and email address.
  • Profile information, including age and gender.
  • Smoking history, cigarettes smoked per day, cigarette brand, cigarette costs, and smoking reduction or quitting goals.
  • Optional target quit date.
  • Cigarette logs, including dates, times, costs, moods, triggers, notes, and any location you manually enter.
  • Subscription status and Premium access information.
  • Technical information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the application.

PureBreath does not request access to your device's GPS location, contacts, or camera. If you manually enter a location in a cigarette log, that information is stored as part of the log.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Save and display your cigarette-tracking history.
  • Calculate progress statistics and estimated money saved.
  • Provide quitting milestones and breathing exercises.
  • Manage Premium subscriptions and access.
  • Maintain application security and troubleshoot problems.

3. Third-Party Services

PureBreath uses the following service providers:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: account registration, sign-in, and authentication.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: storage of account profiles, smoking logs, and app data.
  • RevenueCat: management of subscription status and Premium entitlements.
  • Google Play: application distribution and subscription billing.
  • Expo: application development and delivery infrastructure.

These providers may process information needed to deliver their services, subject to their applicable privacy policies.

4. Advertising and Analytics

PureBreath does not display third-party advertisements and does not intentionally use advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs.

5. Data Storage and Security

Account and smoking-tracking information is stored using Firebase services. We use reasonable measures intended to protect personal information, but no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain account and smoking-tracking information while your account is active or as needed to operate the service, comply with applicable legal obligations, or resolve legitimate disputes.

You can request account deletion through the account-deletion feature inside PureBreath or by emailing spagencyforbusinesses@gmail.com.

When an account is deleted, associated personal profile information and smoking logs are intended to be deleted from our active application databases, subject to any information that must be retained for legal, security, or financial compliance purposes. Copies in service-provider backups may remain temporarily under their retention schedules.

7. Children's Privacy

PureBreath is intended for adults aged 18 and older. It is not designed for children.

8. Your Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law.

9. Health Disclaimer

PureBreath is a smoking-tracking and motivational support application. It does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment, or professional medical advice.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
