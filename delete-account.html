Delete Your PureBreath Account

PureBreath is developed by SP Agency. You can request deletion of your PureBreath account and associated personal data using the methods below.

Option 1: Delete Your Account in the App

  1. Open PureBreath.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Go to your account settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Follow the confirmation instructions.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the app, email spagencyforbusinesses@gmail.com with the subject "PureBreath Account Deletion Request".

Include the email address registered with PureBreath. We may request verification of account ownership. Never send your password.

What Data Is Deleted?

Account deletion is intended to remove your Firebase Authentication account, personal profile information, and associated smoking logs from the active application databases.

What Data May Be Retained?

Some records may be retained where required for legal obligations, payment records, security, or fraud prevention. Service-provider backups may temporarily retain copies according to their retention policies.
